Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to the XiaoZhi backend service (the "Service"). The operator of the Service is the party that actually deploys and manages the Service (the "Operator" or "we"). We understand how important your personal information is to you and will do our utmost to keep it secure.

Please read and fully understand this Privacy Policy before using the Service. By starting to use the Service, you confirm that you have read and agree to this Privacy Policy.

This Privacy Policy applies to our collection, storage, use, sharing and protection of your personal information when you use the Service through the Control Panel (management console), API endpoints or other means.

1. Information We Collect

To provide the Service to you, we may need to collect the following information:

1.1 Account registration information: When you register an account, we collect your mobile phone number or username, password and similar information in order to create and verify your account.

1.2 Device information: When you bind a hardware device, we collect the device identifier (such as device code and MAC address), device model and firmware version in order to manage the device and provide the Service.

1.3 Agent configuration information: When you create and configure an agent, we collect the role template, language model selection, voice parameter configuration and similar settings you provide, in order to deliver personalized AI interaction services.

1.4 Voice interaction data: When you use the voice interaction feature, the Service uses voice activity detection (VAD) to determine where your speech starts and ends, processes your voice input and transmits it to third-party speech recognition (ASR) and language model (LLM) services for processing, in order to provide voice interaction.

1.5 Image data: When you use the vision model feature, we may process the image data captured by your device camera and transmit it to a third-party vision model service for analysis and understanding, in order to provide image recognition, scene understanding and similar functions.

1.6 Conversation memory data: When you enable the memory feature, the Service stores summaries of your interaction history with agents in order to provide a more coherent and personalized experience in later conversations.

1.7 Knowledge base data: When you use the knowledge base feature, we collect and store the documents, text and other knowledge base content you upload so that agents can retrieve knowledge and answer questions during conversations.

1.8 Voiceprint data: When you use the voiceprint recognition feature, we collect and store your voiceprint feature samples for speaker identity verification and personalized services.

1.9 Chat history data: We store the history of your conversations with agents, including the text of the conversation, the time of the conversation and the interaction context, in order to provide a continuous conversation experience and history lookup.

1.10 Conversation audio data: When you use the voice interaction feature, we may store the audio of your interactions with agents in order to improve voice interaction quality and support retrospective lookup.

1.11 Agent configuration data: When you configure an agent, we collect the tags, plugin configuration, context provider settings and similar information you provide, in order to deliver customized agent services.

1.12 Device firmware data: When you use the OTA upgrade feature, we record the device firmware version, upgrade history and similar information for device management and firmware traceability.

1.13 Log information: When you use the Service, we automatically collect service logs, including but not limited to access time, IP address, browser type and operation records, for service operations and security purposes.

1.14 Verification code information: When you log in with your mobile phone number, we send you a verification code through an SMS service for identity verification.

2. How We Use the Information We Collect

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

(1) To provide, maintain and improve the Service, including core functions such as account management, device management and agent configuration.

(2) To process your voice interaction requests by identifying voice input through voice activity detection (VAD) and calling third-party AI model services to perform speech recognition, intent recognition, semantic understanding and speech synthesis.

(3) To process your image data by calling third-party vision model services to perform image recognition and scene understanding.

(4) To store and manage your conversation memory data so as to provide a more coherent experience in later interactions.

(5) To store and retrieve the knowledge base content you upload so that agents can answer your questions more accurately during conversations.

(6) To safeguard the security and stability of the Service, including identity verification, security protection and troubleshooting.

(7) To comply with applicable laws, regulations and regulatory requirements.

We will not use your personal information for other purposes unrelated to those described above. If we need to use the information for other purposes not described in this Privacy Policy, we will obtain your consent in advance.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share or disclose your information only in the following circumstances:

3.1 Third-party service calls: To provide voice interaction, visual understanding and similar functions, your voice data, image data and text content may be transmitted to third-party AI service providers (such as language model, speech recognition, speech synthesis and vision model providers) for processing. We choose providers with reasonable security safeguards, but please be aware that third-party providers will process the data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

3.2 Legal requirements: We may need to disclose your personal information in accordance with applicable laws and regulations, legal proceedings or the requirements of competent government authorities.

3.3 Security safeguards: We may use or disclose personal information to the extent reasonably necessary to protect the rights, property or safety of the Operator, other users or the public from harm.

3.4 With your consent: We may share your personal information with third parties with your explicit consent.

4.1 Storage location: Your personal information is stored on the servers where the Operator deploys the Service, including but not limited to databases (MySQL/PostgreSQL) and cache services (Redis).

4.2 Storage period: We retain your personal information for the period necessary to provide the Service to you. After you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal information within a reasonable period, unless otherwise required by laws or regulations.

(1) Self-hosted: If you deploy the Service yourself, the Operator only provides the code and you are responsible for the actual storage and processing of data. In this case you are both the user of the Service and the controller and protector of the data.

4.6 Cross-border data transfer: When providing intelligent interaction features, the Service may need to call the interfaces of third-party AI service providers outside China. In this process, your voice data, text data, image data and similar information may be transferred to servers outside the People's Republic of China for processing. The overseas providers involved include but are not limited to:

(1) OpenAI: provides speech recognition (Whisper), speech synthesis (TTS), large language model (GPT) and other services.

(2) Anthropic: provides large language model (Claude) services.

(3) Groq: provides speech recognition (Whisper) services.

(4) Microsoft EdgeTTS: provides speech synthesis services.

We will take the necessary measures to safeguard your personal information during cross-border transfer in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China, the Measures for Security Assessment of Data Export and other laws and regulations. The Operator should clearly disclose on the Control Panel whether the third-party services in use involve cross-border data transfer, and inform you of the name, contact details, processing purpose, processing method and categories of personal information of the data recipient. If you do not agree to the transfer of data outside China, you may choose to use only AI services provided by domestic providers (such as Zhipu AI, Alibaba Cloud, Baidu ERNIE and iFlytek) or local deployment options (such as FunASR, FishSpeech and Ollama).

7. Third-Party Services

7.1 When providing intelligent interaction features, the Service needs to call third-party services, including but not limited to:

(1) Voice activity detection service (VAD): detects where voice input starts and ends.

(2) Speech recognition service (ASR): converts your voice input into text.

(3) Large language model service (LLM): performs semantic understanding, intent recognition and reply generation on text.

(4) Speech synthesis service (TTS): converts text replies into voice output.

(5) Vision model service: performs image recognition and understanding.

(6) SMS verification code service: used for identity verification during user registration and login.

(7) MQTT message broker service (MQTT Broker): used for message communication between devices and the server, including delivering device control commands and reporting device status.

(8) Database service: used to persist user data, device data, agent configurations, chat history and similar information.

7.2 The third-party service providers mentioned above process data in accordance with their respective privacy policies. We recommend that you review the privacy policies of the relevant third-party providers before using the Service.
