4. Service Content

4.1 Service overview: Based on artificial intelligence technology, the Service calls third-party generative AI models through API endpoints to provide intelligent interaction services for connected hardware devices, including but not limited to voice activity detection (VAD), speech recognition (ASR), semantic understanding (LLM), speech synthesis (TTS), visual understanding (VLLM), intent recognition (Intent), conversation memory (Memory) and knowledge base (RAG).

4.2 Agent management: Through the Control Panel you can create and configure agents, including setting role templates, selecting language models, configuring voice parameters, setting intent recognition rules, managing knowledge bases, enabling conversation memory and configuring agent plugins. The settings of an agent must not violate national laws and regulations.

4.3 Device management: Through the Control Panel you can bind and manage your hardware devices, and perform device configuration, firmware updates, OTA remote upgrades and other operations. You shall ensure that the devices you manage are lawfully owned by you or have been authorized to you.

4.4 Visual understanding: The Service supports capturing images through the device camera and calling third-party vision models for image recognition and scene understanding. You shall ensure that such image capture complies with applicable laws and regulations and does not infringe the privacy of others.

4.5 Intent recognition: The Service can use intent recognition to understand the intent of user commands and trigger the corresponding operations or services, such as controlling smart devices or querying information.

4.6 Conversation memory: The Service may record and store summaries of your interaction history with agents so as to provide a more coherent and personalized experience in later conversations. You can manage or clear memory data in the Control Panel.

4.7 Knowledge base: The Service allows you to upload documents, text and other content to build knowledge bases, and agents can retrieve knowledge base content during conversations to answer your questions. You shall ensure that the uploaded knowledge base content does not infringe third-party intellectual property rights and does not contain content that violates laws and regulations.

4.8 Voiceprint recognition: The Service supports voiceprint recognition; you may register voiceprint samples for speaker identity verification and personalized services. Voiceprint data will be used for identity verification purposes.

4.9 Voice cloning: The Service supports voice cloning; you may clone a custom voice by providing audio samples. A cloned voice may be used only by you and must not be used to impersonate others or for illegal or non-compliant activities.

4.10 MCP access point: The Service supports the MCP (Model Context Protocol). It can act as an MCP Server to expose tool-calling capabilities to external parties, or as an MCP Client to call external MCP services, enabling standardized integration with external systems.

4.11 Context provider: The Service supports the Context Provider feature, which can obtain real-time information such as weather, news and stock quotes from external data sources, giving agents a richer source of knowledge.

4.12 MQTT protocol: The Service supports the MQTT (Message Queuing Telemetry Transport) protocol for messaging with IoT devices. Through MQTT you can deliver device control commands, monitor device status and more.

4.13 Service description: Conversations and replies generated by the third-party AI models that the Service relies on are for reference only and do not constitute professional advice of any kind. You must not treat the output as professional advice in fields such as medical, legal or financial matters. You bear full responsibility for any judgment or decision you make based on the output.

4.14 Service fees: The Service is a free and open-source project. It does not charge users any usage fees and only provides the platform capability to call third-party services; it does not involve any paid features or value-added services.

The Service supports several AI service providers, whose charging models are as follows:

(1) Free services: Some providers offer free quotas or fully free services, such as Zhipu AI (free models such as glm-4-flash) and Microsoft EdgeTTS speech synthesis.

(2) Pay-as-you-go services: Some providers charge by API call volume, such as OpenAI, Alibaba Cloud Intelligent Speech, Baidu ERNIE and iFlytek. You need to pay the corresponding fees to the third-party providers yourself; such fees are unrelated to the Service.

(3) Local deployment options: The Service supports fully local AI solutions, such as FunASR speech recognition, FishSpeech speech synthesis and Ollama local LLMs. Local deployment incurs no network or API call fees, but requires sufficient local computing resources.

You may choose a suitable service provider according to your actual needs and budget. For specific pricing, please refer to the official documentation of each provider.
