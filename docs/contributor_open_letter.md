# An Open Letter to Developers

"The duck is the first to know when the spring river warms; it is just the time when pufferfish swim upstream!"

Dear friend, I am John, a Java programmer at an ordinary company. Today, with utmost sincerity, I am sending this open letter to you, who loves AI technology and innovation.

Half a year ago, I saw many excellent projects, such as AI-related ones like `Dify` and `Chat2DB`. I thought how wonderful it would be to participate in them—but alas, "no outlet for my ambitions; ten years of code in vain."

In early 2025 I came across videos from Brother Xia's team. I was very curious about how they did it, and I wanted to replicate their backend service and build a low-cost consumer Jarvis. Unfortunately, what I have built so far is still just an "artificial idiot": low concurrency, no soul, slow responses, and many bugs.

Brother Xia's team is our role model. I would love to have a XiaoZhi backend as intelligent as theirs. But I also understand Brother Xia's decision not to open-source it. "One flower does not make spring; a hundred flowers in bloom bring spring to the garden." The era when AI blossoms everywhere may be realized by our generation; with our own hands, we can build a low-cost consumer Jarvis. I personally believe that what they can achieve, we can achieve too—it is only a matter of time. I call this "our pilgrimage."

So what difficulties will we encounter on this pilgrimage? I think no fewer than the eighty-one tribulations. Along the way there will surely be all kinds of demons—and also immortals secretly helping us, and people joining the pilgrimage.

If the above makes you laugh, then I feel very lucky. Being able to make you laugh for five seconds in your 30,000-plus days of life counts as a small contribution to you.

Will the idea of a low-cost consumer Jarvis fail? I do not know. But in the life of ordinary people like us, isn't failure very common?

One thing is certain about the future: someone will fully replicate Brother Xia's team's features and realize a low-cost consumer Jarvis. Will this project be the one?

I look forward to moving forward hand in hand with you to create the future.

John, March 11, 2025, Guangzhou

# Appendix: Development Contribution Guide
## Project Goals

1. **A low-cost consumer Jarvis solution**

2. **A solution for intelligent integration with surrounding hardware**

## Join Us

We warmly welcome like-minded friends to join us and contribute to the project. You can view the features we plan to implement soon at [this link](https://github.com/users/xinnan-tech/projects/3). The items in the feature list that have not yet been assigned to anyone are exactly where your participation is urgently needed. Ways to participate:

### 1. Become a Regular Contributor

Fork the project, submit a PR, and it will be merged into the main branch after review by a developer.

### 2. Become a Developer

After you have submitted 3 valid PRs in total, you can contact the group owner to apply to become a developer. The group owner will invite you to join a dedicated developer group to discuss the project's future together.

## Developer Workflow

1. **Create a new branch**
   Develop each feature on a new branch. Branch names should be concise and clear so that the implemented feature is obvious at a glance, avoiding duplicate work.

2. **Submit a PR for review**
   When feature development is complete, submit a PR on GitHub for review by other developers. After approval, it will be merged into the main branch.
